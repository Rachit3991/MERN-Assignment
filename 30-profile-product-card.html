Design Choices

Colours: Deep blue provides a professional appearance, while orange highlights buttons and important actions.

Card Layout: CSS Grid places the profile and product cards side by side on larger screens.

Rounded Corners: Border-radius creates smooth edges for images, buttons, and cards.

Hover Effects: Cards move slightly upward when the pointer is placed over them.

Responsive Design: Media queries stack the cards vertically on smaller screens.

Reusability: Shared CSS classes and variables keep the design consistent and easier to maintain.
