Responsive Audit Checklist

  • Viewport meta tag included.
  • Fixed-width container replaced with a flexible container.
  • Images resize to fit their containers.
  • Headings scale using clamp().
  • Navigation links wrap on narrow screens.
  • Grid columns adapt to the available width.
  • Wide tables scroll inside their wrapper.
  • Navigation links have a minimum height of 44px.
